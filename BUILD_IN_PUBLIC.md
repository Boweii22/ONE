# ONE — Build in public playbook

Build in Public is not won by repeatedly posting screenshots. The story is: **bold hypothesis → real test → uncomfortable evidence → visible decision → working proof**.

## The public story

1. **Day 1 — The bet:** “What happens if the whole internet gets only one post?” Show the first rough prototype and state the risky assumption.
2. **Day 2 — The failed test:** publish the anonymised WhatsApp numbers: 15 invited, about 50 attempts, seven active, 15 ownership announcements, zero organic spread. Explain that bidding was not the fun part.
3. **Day 3 — The pivot:** announce free takeovers, cooldown fairness, and optional Revenge Tickets. Ask followers to predict whether revenge creates retention.
4. **Day 4 — The hard engineering:** screen-record two devices hitting Take together. Explain “one winner, no charge for the loser” in normal language.
5. **Day 5 — The world can watch:** launch the public web mirror and post the live URL, not a mock-up.
6. **Day 6 — The emotional loop:** show a genuine OneSignal dethroned notification and the one-tap rematch.
7. **Day 7 — Revenue without ruining it:** show RevenueCat’s server webhook crediting a Revenge Ticket and explain why ownership remains free.
8. **Launch — The event:** schedule a one-hour global takeover war, publish live numbers, and let the community choose the winning message.

## What to publish each day

- One short vertical video showing a real behaviour on physical devices.
- One exact metric or engineering fact.
- One honest question that lets people influence tomorrow’s build.
- One link to the live product or test signup.
- One reply thread containing what changed because of feedback.

Never fake users, views, revenue, testimonials, or “viral” numbers. Small real numbers plus a sharp decision are more credible than giant simulated counters.

## Metrics worth showing

- Unique viewers and live watchers.
- Successful takeovers and stale-race attempts.
- Median reign duration.
- Percentage of dethroned users who return within ten minutes.
- Revenge Tickets earned, purchased, and spent.
- Share-link visits that become app installs.
- Reports per 1,000 messages and moderation response time.

## Launch hooks

- “Take the only screen on Earth.”
- “I owned the whole app for 43 seconds.”
- “There are 8 billion people and one post.”
- “Steal this from me.”
- “Two phones pressed at once. Only one became the internet.”

## Evidence folder

Keep dated, unedited proof for the submission: raw screen recordings, analytics exports, Git commits, public post links, user quotes with permission, test notes, moderation decisions, and receipts from real multi-device races. Judges should be able to trace every major claim back to evidence.
